MindWatch 隱私權政策

English · 最後更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日

MindWatch 把你的心情記錄、筆記、計畫、小行動回饋，以及從 Apple「健康」整理出的每日摘要，只存在你的裝置上。開發者和任何第三方都收不到這些資料。App 沒有帳號，也沒有分析或廣告追蹤。

「健康」資料

只有在你允許後，MindWatch 才會讀取「健康」資料，用來和你自己的平常狀態比較。MindWatch 不會寫入「健康」，也不會把健康資料用於廣告。原始健康樣本不會被保存，只保留每日摘要。

刪除資料

到「設定 → 刪除本機資料」可以刪除 MindWatch 的所有資料；刪除 App 也會一併移除。Apple「健康」裡的資料不受影響。

聯絡

有問題請到 GitHub Issues 留言。

MindWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

MindWatch stores your check-ins, notes, plans, action feedback, and daily summaries derived from Apple Health only on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. The app has no account, analytics, or ad tracking.

Health data

Health data is read only with your permission and is used to compare with your own baseline. MindWatch does not write to Health and never uses Health data for advertising. Raw Health samples are not stored; only daily summaries are kept.

Deleting data

Delete all MindWatch data in Settings → Delete local data; deleting the app also removes it. Data in Apple Health is not changed.
